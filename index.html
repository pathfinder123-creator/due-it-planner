A little planning, less remembering

Add an item with a date. Time and details are optional. Use steps for the smaller actions leading up to it.

Templates: choose a type and action to open a starting item with an editable checklist. Set your own date and details, then save it to your planner. Step dates and times are optional.

Counts: Overdue includes past dates and elapsed times today. Today includes the remaining items and steps dated today. Coming up includes tomorrow through your selected number of days. Each dated item or step is counted once, including work sessions. Undated steps do not enter the counts.

Needs attention shows overdue items, today's items, those coming soon, and items inside their individual reminder window. Counts update while you use the tool and when you return. There are no notifications outside the tool.

Progress is the number of completed steps. Check off the main item separately. Finishing a repeating item creates its next occurrence and resets its checklist. Weekly repeats use the selected weekday; monthly weekday repeats use its position in the month (for example, the second Tuesday). A fifth weekday is treated as the last weekday. An end date includes occurrences on that date and stops new ones afterward.

Calendar: select items and download a calendar file, then open or import it in your calendar app. Dated steps can be exported separately from their project. Calendar apps may handle reminders differently; check the imported event. Exports do not synchronize later edits.

Sharing: select items and preview their details before sending. Sharing does not create a shared planner.

Your data: this planner uses this browser's storage, including when offline after installation. It does not automatically sync between devices. Back up regularly; clearing browser data can remove entries. Load backup opens a saved backup to continue on this device. This replaces your current planner after confirmation.

Install: on iPhone/iPad use Safari's Share menu, then Add to Home Screen. On supported desktop/Android browsers, use Install app or the browser's install menu. Installation requires a hosted HTTPS address, not opening a downloaded HTML file.
